The route begins on the Adriatic coast in Monopoli, builds through Puglia’s white towns and sea caves, then crosses the country with stops in Alberobello and Pompeii to Positano.

From there, the family takes to the water for Amalfi and Capri before moving north to Rome for fountains, piazzas, the Colosseum, and a proper grand finale.

A few
route realities.

  • For the Lecce + Baia dei Turchi day, plan a car/taxi transfer after Lecce; Baia dei Turchi is near Otranto, not walkable from the station.
  • Polignano’s San Vito boat departure is outside the historic center — confirm the operator’s exact meeting point and allow local transfer time.
  • Monopoli → Alberobello → Pompeii → Positano is a full travel day. Keep the Pompeii stop timed and pre-booked.
  • Amalfi and Capri crossings are weather-dependent. Check the operating port and final sailing time the day before.
  • Positano is vertical: pack light for day outings and leave margin for stairs, port transfers, and luggage handling.
  • Rome’s historic center is easiest on foot, while the Colosseum/Forum visit benefits from a timed entry reservation.

Timing cues are planning guidance, not live transport schedules. Reconfirm boats, rail departures, opening hours, and restaurant reservations close to travel.
